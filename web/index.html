    How the audit works

    Language models read; a theorem prover decides. Nemotron turns each policy into logic, every clause tied to a sentence of the policy. Z3 then checks that logic against Colorado and federal law, exhaustively within a stated range (1–12 people, income up to $1M, first bill by day 400). How faithful the logic is to the policy is measured separately, below. No model ever says whether a policy is legal.

    1. FindTavily searches the web; Nemotron 3 Nano picks the hospital's own document; code fetches it.
    2. FormaliseNemotron 3 Ultra writes the policy as rules over household facts (income, size, insurance, residency…) and deadlines.
    3. Check the translationCode keeps a clause only if its quote is in the policy; Z3 rejects rules that can never apply; Nemotron 3 Super checks each quote says what the clause claims.
    4. Second opinionNemotron 3 Super formalises the policy on its own, five separate times. Z3 replays each counterexample's exact household or day in every Super model; it is confirmed only when at least three fail too. A model that lost rules in checking can't confirm a gap its own missing rule could explain.
    5. ProveZ3 checks ten legal requirements. A pass is a proof about the encoded rules within the stated range; a fail is a concrete household or day.

    What the audit can't do

    • It audits the written policy. A hospital may apply Colorado's law through a process its policy doesn't describe, so a counterexample means "the policy as written doesn't guarantee this", not "the hospital breaks the law".
    • The translation can be wrong. Every finding links to the policy's own words; disputed findings (most of Super's five models don't fail for the same case) need a person to read the policy.
    • Household facts the vocabulary can't express (specific services, documents, procedures) are left out and noted, never guessed.
    • Only Colorado law and federal 501(r) are encoded. Policies change: each audit shows the document it used.
    • Some hospital sites block downloads; those hospitals are missing, not passed.
    • Not legal advice.
