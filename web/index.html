    How Waive decides

    The AI reads; code decides. Nemotron models turn bills and policies into data. Plain code checks every quote against the policy text and does all the maths. A rule we can't prove never affects your answer.

    Read the billNemotron 3.5 Super VL reads the photo; code checks the totals and looks for duplicate lines.
    Find the policyTavily searches the web; Nemotron 3 Nano picks the hospital's own document; code fetches it.
    CompileNemotron 3 Ultra turns the policy into rules, each with a quote. Nemotron 3 Super compiles it again on its own.
    ProveCode drops any rule whose quote isn't in the text. Where the two models disagree, Ultra settles it with a quote.
    DecideCode computes your % of the poverty line, the tier, the amount, Colorado's cap and your deadline.

    What Waive can't do (yet)

    • Only Colorado hospitals are pre-compiled. Elsewhere Waive searches live and applies only the federal 501(r) rules, not your state's law.
    • It can't see a hospital's internal procedures: asset tests and "service area" rules are flagged for you to confirm, not assumed.
    • Doctors' groups often bill separately and may not be covered by the hospital's policy. Waive says so when the policy does.
    • Colorado's cap is shown as a maximum. The state's discounted rates can lower it further; Waive can't compute those rates.
    • Policies change. Each Atlas entry shows the date we fetched the document. Always confirm with the hospital.
    • This is not legal advice and Waive never contacts anyone for you.
